Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our club and events.

Do I need coding experience to join?

Not at all! We welcome designers, product managers, and absolute beginners. Mentors are on-site to help you learn.

Are hackathons mandatory?

Attend as many or as few as you like. We meet weekly for workshops and host official hackathons once a month.

Is there any cost to participate?

Zero. All events, food, swag, and cloud credits are 100% free for registered students.

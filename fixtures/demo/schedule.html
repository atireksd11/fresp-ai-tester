Hackathon Timeline & Events

Here is how a typical 12-hour weekend sprint runs from kickoff to demo.

Hour 00 - 02

Kickoff & Team Formation

Opening remarks, challenge statements unveiled, and lightning pitch sessions to form teams of 2-4 hackers.

Hour 02 - 08

Hacking & Mentorship

Head down coding with pizza delivery, coffee refills, and roving industry mentors assisting with technical hurdles.

Hour 08 - 10

Submission & Polish

Code freeze, GitHub repository cleanup, and recording a 2-minute Loom video demo for judges.

Hour 10 - 12

Demo Fair & Awards

Science-fair style judging booths, live demos, closing remarks, and grand prize announcements.
